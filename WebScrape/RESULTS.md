## Recall — independent golden sets

Golden sets (`golden/golden_<inst>.csv`) were built from web search, institute news,
annual reports, faculty pages and arXiv PDFs, **without** any of the bibliographic APIs used by the pipeline.

| | Ropar | Kharagpur | Roorkee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Golden papers | 339 | 637 | 562 |
| Recall, all golden papers | 77.9 % | 67.0 % | 73.7 % |
| Recall, golden papers that have a DOI | **83.1 %** | **81.1 %** | **80.6 %** |
| Recall if the excluded low-confidence candidates were added | 83.8 % | 73.0 % | 79.4 % |

Recall by golden-set source (found / n):

| Source | Ropar | Kharagpur | Roorkee |
|---|---|---|---|
| faculty pages | 107/137 | 234/391 | 274/380 |
| department pages | 32/39 | 56/58 | 80/111 |
| arXiv | 37/47 | 72/102 | 49/51 |
| annual reports | 85/112 | 9/21 | 1/6 |
| news / web search | 3/4 | 56/65 | 10/14 |

Most misses without a DOI are grey literature (conference abstracts, seminar talks, magazine articles)
that no bibliographic index covers. Misses with a DOI are mostly Elsevier/IEEE papers by faculty with
very common names, where no open source exposes the affiliation.

† audited before the namesake fixes (PROCESS.md §9); Roorkee audited on final data, but 32/40 likely
records could not be checked (publisher sites block scripted access). **Verified records are reliable;
likely records are not reliably measured and should be treated as TODO.**

## Validation (all records, all three files)

* 0 violations on every structural check: schema, year range, DOI format/uniqueness, ISSN format,
  citation values, references are DOIs, no self-references, `todo_fields` consistency, and every
  verified record's matched affiliation re-classifies as the IIT.
* Live re-fetch of 200 random records per institute from Crossref: title, year and venue match in
  99–100 % of cases.
* Citation counts, Crossref vs OpenCitations: Spearman ρ = 0.83 / 0.87 / 0.88, median difference 0.
* Remaining same-title pairs (22 / 80 / 48) are mostly a conference paper and its later journal version
  (distinct publications, kept).
