    Network dynamics

    How the network grew, where it breaks, how ideas spread.

    The faculty network of both IITs together, studied as a dynamical system: its growth year by year, its robustness when faculty leave, and SIR spreading along co-author ties.

    Growth, 2016–2026

    The network at the end of each year, with each tie dated by the pair's first joint paper.

    Robustness when faculty leave

    Share of all faculty in the largest connected group as faculty are removed: at random, or the most connected first (degree), or the biggest bridges first (betweenness), re-ranked after every step.

    SIR spreading

    An idea passes along each co-author tie with probability T, then the carrier stops (SIR). This equals bond percolation, so outbreaks are exact cluster sizes, averaged over 300 samples.

    Who spreads best

    How well each centrality predicts the outbreak a faculty member starts at T = 0.2 (Spearman ρ). The k-core wins, as Kitsak et al. found.

    Method

    How this was built.

    Papers

    Scraped from Crossref, Europe PMC, DOAJ, INSPIRE, ORCID, DBLP and Semantic Scholar, then checked against OpenAlex. A paper counts only when the IIT is named in an author's affiliation on the paper itself.

    Faculty

    Papers are linked to faculty from the institutes' own lists by ORCID, then by name and department. Unclear names are left unlinked rather than guessed; about 83% of papers have a faculty author.

    Journals and conferences

    Journal quartiles come from Scimago for the year of publication. Conference ranks come from the CORE portal edition in force that year.

    Networks

    Citation links and Indian partners come from OpenAlex. Influence uses PageRank (damping 0.85), also without self-citations and rescaled by age; communities use Louvain over ten random seeds, checked with Leiden. Robustness and SIR spreading are simulated on the faculty network.

    Read with care

    h-index and i10 here cover 2016–2026 papers only, so they are about half a career value. IF is OpenAlex's 2-year mean citedness, which runs higher than Clarivate's JIF. Citation counts are as of October 2026. PageRank favours older papers, and 88% of citations inside an institute are self-citations.

    Scope

    Science and engineering only: papers whose OpenAlex topic is in the Physical or Life Sciences. Faculty of humanities, management, law, education and medical units are not included.
