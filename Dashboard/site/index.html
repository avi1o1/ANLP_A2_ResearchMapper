        Method

        How this was built.

        Papers

        Scraped from Crossref, Europe PMC, DOAJ, INSPIRE, ORCID, DBLP and Semantic Scholar, then checked against OpenAlex. A paper counts only when the IIT is named in an author's affiliation on the paper itself.

        Faculty

        Papers are linked to faculty from the institutes' own lists by ORCID, then by name and department. Unclear names are left unlinked rather than guessed; about 83% of papers have a faculty author.

        Journals and conferences

        Journal quartiles come from Scimago for the year of publication. Conference ranks come from the CORE portal edition in force that year.

        Networks

        Citation links and Indian partners come from OpenAlex. Influence uses PageRank (damping 0.85); communities use Louvain over ten random seeds.

        Read with care

        h-index and i10 here cover 2016–2026 papers only, so they are about half a career value. IF is OpenAlex's 2-year mean citedness, which runs higher than Clarivate's JIF. Citation counts are as of October 2026. PageRank favours older papers.

        Scope

        Science and engineering only: papers whose OpenAlex topic is in the Physical or Life Sciences. Faculty of humanities, management, law, education and medical units are not included.
